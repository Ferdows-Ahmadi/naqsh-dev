01 / Research

Applied AI with evidence behind the answer.

My research interest is not just whether a model predicts correctly, but whether it generalizes, focuses on meaningful evidence, and can be trusted enough to be useful.

Undergraduate Thesis

Computer-Aided Tuberculosis Detection from Chest X-Ray Images Using Machine Learning Techniques

I developed a lung-aware deep-learning pipeline for tuberculosis detection from chest X-rays, combining lung segmentation, classification, cross-dataset evaluation, and explainability through Grad-CAM. The work focuses on the difficult part of medical AI: generalizing beyond a single dataset while keeping model attention clinically meaningful.
