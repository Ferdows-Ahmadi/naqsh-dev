Research

Applied AI should do more than produce a prediction. It should earn enough trust to be examined, challenged, and improved.

My undergraduate research focuses on computer-aided tuberculosis detection from chest X-ray images. The work combines lung-aware preprocessing, deep-learning classification, cross-dataset evaluation, and explainability so model behavior can be inspected instead of treated as a black box.
